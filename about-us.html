How we came
to be here.

Draw Clever draws houses, and the interiors that go in them, from ateliers in Romania and Spain. The work is mostly residential, with commercial commissions taken when they can have the same attention.

Every project begins with listening. From the first sketch to the final handover, one team carries the architecture and the interiors. Nothing is referred out, so nothing arrives back a stranger.

What we
hold to.

Four rules. They settle more of the work than taste does.

01

Restraint

The discipline of removing, until what remains is essential and exact.

02

Materiality

Honest materials, honestly detailed. Stone and timber authored as carefully as the geometry, and light as carefully as either.

03

Permanence

Buildings designed to be inherited, not refurbished. A century is our minimum horizon.

04

Discretion

The work speaks first. We let the architecture introduce the client.
