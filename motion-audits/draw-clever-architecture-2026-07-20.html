Draw Clever Architecture — a luxury brand site whose motion now matches its restraint

Multi-page Vite site for a luxury architecture & interior design atelier: ink-black canvas, gold ramp, monumental serif display type, render photography. Motion audited and reworked in the same pass; findings below record what was flagged and the shipped fix.

Brand / marketing site · vanilla JS + CSS (no framework) · Vercel
0Critical 4Important 3Opportunities
Overall

The site's motion vocabulary was already unusually strong for a hand-built page — masked line-rise headlines, a FLIP portfolio filter, cross-document view transitions. Its weaknesses were uniformity (one identical fade-up applied to fifty-one blocks), a handful of layout-property animations, and a gallery that opened with no spatial connection to what you clicked. All four Important findings were fixed in this pass; what remains is deliberate, differentiated, and honors reduced motion throughout.

  • 6 Image develop-in 900–1000ms · scrubbed/eased

What's off: nothing that isn't chosen. Everything a visitor interacts with resolves under 560ms. The two entries past the 600ms line are image cinematography — the develop-in is scroll-scrubbed (progress-driven, not a wait) and the hover zoom is a slow luxury drift rather than a response the user waits on. For this register that pacing is the brand; in a productivity tool it would be a finding.
03 · Primary lens

Jakub Krehel — Production Polish

Primary

Strong — the polish layer is real, and the two production tells found here are fixed.

What's working well
  • Masked line-rise headlines (src/site.css .rl) — a distinct, on-brand entrance that generalises the hero's choreography instead of repeating a generic fade.
  • Portfolio filter is a true FLIP (projects.html): leaving cards fade, survivors glide to their new grid slots, entrants stagger in — with an instant fallback under reduced motion.
  • LQIP blur-up on every card and interlude (src/site.css .pc picture::before) — images materialize from a real placeholder, never pop from blank.
  • Exits are subtler than enters throughout: the lightbox closes with a quick fade while opening gets the full shared-element morph.
  • Hover states all transition (150–350ms) — nothing snaps.
Issues to address
Important · fixed

One identical fade-up applied to 51 blocks

WhatEvery .reveal — prose, forms, image grids, offices, FAQ — shared the same opacity + translateY(26px) entrance. That uniformity is the single most recognisable AI-generated-page fingerprint of 2026.

Why it mattersWhen everything moves identically, motion stops carrying information. Context should drive the treatment: photography deserves cinema, body copy deserves to just appear.

Recommended motion (shipped)Prose now crossfades in place (550ms, no translate). Image cards and full-bleeds "develop": the picture settles from a 1.045 zoom while brightness ramps up — transform/filter only. Headlines keep their masked line-rise.

Important · fixed

Gallery opened with no spatial link to the clicked card

WhatClicking a project card faded the lightbox in centered — the card you clicked and the photo that appeared had no visual relationship.

Why it mattersShared-element continuity is the highest-value motion a gallery can have: it tells the visitor this grew from that, keeps orientation, and reads as craft.

Recommended motion (shipped)A fixed clone of the card's image morphs rect-to-rect into the stage (560ms). Wrapper scale and image counter-scale are sampled as paired keyframes so pixels are never stretched; the clone hands off to the full-resolution image once it has faded in. Reduced motion and missing rects keep the plain crossfade.

05 · Selective lens

Emil Kowalski — Restraint & Speed

Selective

What's working well
  • Form submit gives instant state feedback ("Sending…", disabled) — no decorative delay on the one high-stakes action.

Issues to address
Important · fixed

Layout properties animated on six interaction surfaces

WhatProgress bar animated width, nav underline grew via width, brand logo shrank via height, burger lines moved via top, links opened gap, the accordion animated max-height (with a 260px clip risk on long answers).

Why it mattersLayout-property animation forces reflow — worst on the progress bar, which updated on every scroll frame. Transform-based equivalents run on the compositor.

Recommended motion (shipped)scaleX for progress and underline, scale for the brand, translateY for the burger, arrow-only movement for links, and grid-template-rows: 0fr→1fr for the accordion (which also removes the clip risk).
